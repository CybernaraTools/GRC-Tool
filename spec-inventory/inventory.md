Model, CMMI (Capability Maturity Model Integration) is a process improvement framework — not a technical compliance standard or a cybersecurity control framework. ISACA, which now stewards CMMI, describes it as a model for improving organisational performance and capability maturity across people, processes, and technology.

It is fundamentally different from frameworks such as ISO 27001, NIST CSF, SOC 2, or PCI-DSS, which prescribe specific controls and are used for external audit and certification. CMMI instead describes how well an organisation's processes are defined, managed, measured, and optimised — and uses appraisals (not audits) to assess that maturity.

Key implication for GRC teams: Do NOT treat this checklist as a list of controls to tick off. Instead, use it to evaluate process capability and identify improvement opportunities.
