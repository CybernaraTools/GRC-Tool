1. Executive Summary
Cybernara will provide a private-cloud compliance operating system for mid-market and enterprise organizations. The product will
normalize regulatory and standards content, map overlapping obligations to reusable harmonized controls, coordinate evidence
and remediation, automate continuous assurance, and generate audit-ready PDF and Excel outputs. Privacy obligations and
operational privacy workflows are first-class alongside security, healthcare, quality, and maturity frameworks.
Control harmonization is deterministic, versioned, source-traceable, and subject to expert approval. Governed AI accelerates
question generation, evidence classification, policy work, regulatory impact analysis, and questionnaire responses, but it cannot
silently change authoritative content, applicability, scores, approvals, or conclusions.
Product decision
Master position
Target customers
Paid mid-market and enterprise customers with private-cloud deployment and customer-controlled data boundaries.
Initial framework scope
GDPR, DPDP Act and Rules, CCPA/CPRA, Saudi PDPL, HIPAA, SOC 2, ISO 27001, NIST SP 800-53, Essential Eight,
CMMI and ISO 9001; extensible framework packs support future standards.
Primary users
GRC administrators, privacy/legal teams, CISOs, control owners, evidence owners, risk managers, auditors, executives,
vendor managers and platform administrators.
Primary outputs
Live compliance posture, control/evidence workspaces, findings and risk registers, regulatory impact, audit packages,
signed PDF and Excel reports, APIs and trust artifacts.
Product boundary
Workflow and assurance software; not legal advice or a substitute for an accredited auditor or authorized legal
interpretation.

2. Problem, Vision, and Outcomes
Problem
Required outcome
Duplicated obligations and evidence across frameworks
A single harmonized control can satisfy many mapped requirements while unique obligations
remain explicit.
Spreadsheet-based, stale compliance programs
Versioned workflows, ownership, continuous tests, alerts and evidence freshness replace periodic
manual reconciliation.
Weak source traceability and legal governance
Every obligation, mapping, question and report conclusion retains citations, version, rationale and
approval history.
Slow audit and customer assurance
Reusable evidence, auditor workspaces, questionnaire automation and trust-center publishing
reduce manual response time.
Fragmented privacy operations
Data inventory, RoPA, DPIA, rights requests, consent, retention, transfer and incident workflows
connect to controls and risk.
Uncontrolled use of AI
A provider-neutral, evaluated, human-governed AI layer preserves privacy, consistency,
reproducibility and safe fallback.
Goals

Establish one authoritative compliance graph spanning requirements, controls, assets, processing activities, vendors, evidence, risks,
policies, owners and reports.

Reduce duplicate work through mapping and evidence reuse without overstating coverage.

Provide enterprise administration, private-cloud isolation, customer-controlled encryption and auditability.

Make new framework versions and customer-specific frameworks configurable content releases rather than software rewrites.
Non-goals for initial release

Providing binding legal advice, certification or audit opinion.

Replacing SIEM, EDR, IAM, ticketing, data catalog or consent execution engines where integration is more appropriate.

Allowing autonomous AI to approve mappings, evidence, risk acceptance or compliance conclusions.

Target

19. Core Glossary
Term
Definition
Requirement
A source obligation or criterion from a specific framework version.
Harmonized control
A reusable control objective mapped to one or more requirements.
Control instance
The assessment-specific execution of a harmonized control within an approved scope.
Question version
An immutable curated or approved AI-assisted question set used by assessment items.
Evidence object
A protected artifact or automated observation with provenance, scope, period and integrity hash.
Assessment snapshot
The frozen framework, mapping, scope, question, answer and conclusion state used for reporting.
