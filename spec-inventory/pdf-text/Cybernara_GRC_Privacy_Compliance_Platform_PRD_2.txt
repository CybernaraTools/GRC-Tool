1. Executive Summary
Cybernara will build a compliance operating platform that converts framework-heavy spreadsheet content into a guided,
evidence-driven system of record. The platform will help organizations understand which obligations apply, assess their
current posture, coordinate remediation, and generate audit-ready outputs across privacy and security frameworks.
The product will separate framework-specific legal or audit requirements from reusable harmonized controls. This split is
central to scalability: one implemented control can satisfy many mapped requirements, while framework-specific
differences remain traceable for legal, audit, and regional interpretation.

3. Problem Statement
Organizations face overlapping privacy and security obligations that are often maintained in separate spreadsheets, tools,
policies, and audit trackers. This creates duplicate control work, inconsistent evidence, weak traceability, and slow
response to regulatory change.
●
Frameworks use different terminology for similar obligations, such as access control, retention, breach response,
consent, vendor management, encryption, and audit logging.
●
Compliance teams need a single way to map obligations to controls, owners, evidence, risks, findings, remediation tasks,
and reporting outputs.
●
A future-ready product must support new frameworks without redesigning the core assessment, evidence, workflow, or
reporting capabilities.

6. Framework Support Model
The product must distinguish three layers: framework requirements, harmonized controls, and operational
implementation. This allows GDPR, DPDP, CCPA, PDPL, HIPAA, SOC 2, ISO 27001, NIST, Essential Eight, CMMI, and
ISO 9001 to share reusable operational controls while preserving framework-specific citations and obligations.
Layer
Purpose
Examples

article, clause, or control catalog.

Harmonized control
Reusable control statement that can satisfy multiple
mapped requirements.

10. Data And Domain Model
The platform should be designed around a clean domain model with explicit aggregates. This supports SOLID design,
high cohesion, low coupling, independent testing, and future services such as AI mapping, continuous controls
monitoring, and regulatory intelligence.
Domain Object
Description
Framework
Regulation, standard, or model with name, jurisdiction, owner, effective dates, version, source, and status.
Requirement
Atomic obligation or control item from a framework, preserving citation, text, category, applicability rules, and
penalties.
HarmonizedControl
Reusable control mapped to one or more requirements with domain, objective, implementation guidance, and
evidence expectations.
ControlMapping
Relationship between requirement and harmonized control with coverage, confidence, rationale, source row, and
reviewer status.
Assessment
Scoped compliance evaluation across frameworks, systems, business units, regions, and time periods.
ControlInstance
Organization-specific implementation of a harmonized control within an assessment scope.
Evidence
Files, URLs, attestations, API-collected artifacts, metadata, validity period, classification, and review trail.
Finding
Gap, deficiency, risk, or exception linked to requirement, control, evidence, test, and remediation.
Task
Work item for owner execution, review, escalation, approval, or remediation.
Report
Generated output with selected scope, framework, evidence, findings, and approval history.
